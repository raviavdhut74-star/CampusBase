CampusBase — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

CampusBase is a study-material app for engineering students (notes, question banks, previous year papers). This page explains what information the app uses.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, password (stored hashed by our authentication provider), PRN, university, branch and year that you enter when you register.
  • Usage data: the files you open or download inside the app (to show your Downloads list).

How we use it

To create your account, show you the study material for your university, branch and semester, and let administrators manage content. We do not sell your information and we do not show third-party ads.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with our backend provider (Supabase). Files are served over HTTPS.

Your choices

You can edit your profile inside the app. To delete your account and data, contact us at the email below.
